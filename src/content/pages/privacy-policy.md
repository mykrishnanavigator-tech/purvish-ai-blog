WiseHoots.ai is a personal website and blog about practical AI projects, learning, and product experiments. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, use contact or comment features, or interact with linked projects such as apps, demos, and external services.

This policy is written for transparency and is not legal advice.

## Information Collected

WiseHoots.ai may collect information in the following ways:

- **Information you provide:** If you use a contact form, send a message, subscribe to updates, or otherwise reach out, the site may receive your name, email address, message content, and any other details you choose to include.
- **Technical information:** Like most websites, the hosting provider, browser, or security systems may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, pages visited, dates and times of access, and basic diagnostic logs.
- **Comments and embedded services:** Blog comments, embedded media, app store links, demos, or third-party widgets may be provided by external services. Those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.
- **AI project interactions:** Some linked apps or demos may use AI services to process prompts, messages, uploaded files, or other inputs you provide. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information into a demo unless the specific app or service says it is designed to handle that data securely.

## How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Respond to messages, questions, or support requests.
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
- Understand which content is useful to visitors.
- Detect abuse, spam, security issues, or technical problems.
- Develop, test, and improve AI-related projects and demos, where applicable.
- Comply with legal obligations or enforce site policies.

## Cookies and Local Storage

The website may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies for basic functionality, preferences, comments, security, and embedded third-party services.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site features, including comments, embedded content, preferences, or forms.

## Third-Party Services

WiseHoots.ai may link to or embed third-party services, including app stores, hosting platforms, AI providers, comment systems, analytics providers, media platforms, and project demos hosted outside this website.

When you leave WiseHoots.ai or interact with an embedded third-party service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply. WiseHoots.ai is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites, platforms, or services.

## Sharing of Information

Personal information is not sold. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the website or connected services through trusted service providers.
- Respond to your request or deliver a feature you chose to use.
- Protect the website, users, or the public from abuse or security threats.
- Comply with law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

## Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Messages, logs, comments, and analytics records may be retained for operational, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

## Your Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding personal information. You may also opt out of some browser-based tracking by changing browser settings or using privacy controls provided by third-party services.

To make a privacy request, use the contact page and include enough detail to identify the information involved.

## Children's Privacy

WiseHoots.ai is not intended for children under 13. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please use the contact page so the information can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no website, app, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## International Visitors

If you access WiseHoots.ai from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where service providers operate. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws in your country.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.
